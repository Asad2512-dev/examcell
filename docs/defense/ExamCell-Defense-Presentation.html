13 / 28
Defense

Grading calculation

Weighted percentage = sum of (score ÷ maximum × weight)

Illustration: 16/20 × 20 + 60/80 × 80 = 76%

Approved boundaries determine grade and grade points

Quality points = course credits × grade points

GPA = total quality points ÷ total credits
